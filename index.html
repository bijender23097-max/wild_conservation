Protect Wildlife. Save Nature.

Many animals in India are in danger. Let us learn about them and help to save them.

Endangered Animals

Bengal Tiger

Status: Endangered

India's national animal. It is in danger because of poaching and loss of forests.

Asian Elephant

Status: Endangered

Elephants need big forests to live. Forests are getting smaller every year.

Indian Rhinoceros

Status: Vulnerable

Mostly found in Kaziranga National Park, Assam.

Snow Leopard

Status: Vulnerable

Lives in the cold Himalayan mountains. It is very hard to spot.

How You Can Help

  • Plant more trees.
  • Do not use plastic bags.
  • Never buy items made from animal skin.
  • Do not throw garbage in forests and rivers.
  • Spread awareness with your friends.
